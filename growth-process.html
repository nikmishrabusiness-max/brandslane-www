This brand specialize in crafting trend-setting dresses that seamlessly blend classic western elements with contemporary flair, appealing to fashion-forward women worldwide.

Something beyond just revenue, their messaging was crafted very diligently for the ads which amplified their USPs and help connect with the audience emotionally. The brand is well on its ways to start scaling aggressively to aim for ₹1 Cr. monthly revenue.
